EMERGENCY FUND

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses or financial disruptions.

PURPOSE

An emergency fund can help cover unexpected costs such as:

- Medical expenses
- Urgent repairs
- Unexpected bills
- Temporary loss of income
- Other financial emergencies

ACCESSIBILITY

Emergency savings are generally intended to be accessible when needed.

Money needed for emergencies is usually different from money intended for long-term investing because investments can fluctuate in value.

HOW MUCH TO SAVE

The appropriate emergency fund size depends on factors such as:

- Monthly essential expenses
- Income stability
- Employment situation
- Dependents
- Insurance coverage
- Expected financial obligations

A common approach is to build emergency savings gradually based on essential living expenses.

BUILDING AN EMERGENCY FUND

An emergency fund can be built through regular contributions.

For example, a person could allocate part of each paycheck toward emergency savings.

Automating contributions can make consistent saving easier.

WHERE TO KEEP IT

Emergency savings should generally be kept somewhere safe and reasonably accessible.

The purpose of an emergency fund is stability and access rather than maximizing investment returns.

EMERGENCY FUND AND INVESTMENTS

Emergency savings and long-term investments serve different purposes.

Emergency savings are designed for unexpected near-term needs, while investments are generally intended for longer-term financial goals and can lose value.

REVIEWING THE FUND

An emergency fund should be reviewed when income, expenses, employment, household responsibilities, or major financial obligations change.

IMPORTANT

The appropriate emergency fund amount varies between individuals and depends on their financial circumstances.
