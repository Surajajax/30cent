CREDIT AND DEBT

Debt is money borrowed from another person or organization that must generally be repaid according to agreed terms.

COMMON TYPES OF DEBT

Examples of debt include:

- Credit card debt
- Personal loans
- Student loans
- Auto loans
- Mortgages

INTEREST

Interest is the cost of borrowing money.

A higher interest rate generally increases the amount paid over time when a balance remains outstanding.

CREDIT CARDS

A credit card allows a person to borrow money for purchases up to a specified credit limit.

Credit card balances may accrue interest according to the card's terms.

PAYING CREDIT CARD BALANCES

Paying the statement balance in full by the due date can help avoid interest charges on purchases when the card's terms provide a grace period.

Making only minimum payments can cause a balance to remain outstanding for a longer period and can increase total interest costs.

CREDIT HISTORY

Credit history records information about borrowing and repayment activity.

Payment history, amounts owed, length of credit history, types of credit, and applications for new credit can affect credit-related assessments.

DEBT MANAGEMENT

Managing debt involves understanding:

- Outstanding balances
- Interest rates
- Minimum payments
- Due dates
- Repayment periods

High-interest debt can become expensive when balances remain unpaid for long periods.

A person considering debt repayment strategies should consider their income, expenses, interest rates, and financial goals.

IMPORTANT

Debt decisions depend on individual financial circumstances and the terms of the specific debt.
