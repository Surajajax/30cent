PERSONAL FINANCE BASICS

Personal finance is the management of money, including income, spending, saving, borrowing, and investing.

INCOME

Income is money received by a person. Common examples include salary, wages, freelance income, business income, and investment income.

EXPENSES

Expenses are amounts of money spent on goods and services. Expenses can be fixed or variable.

FIXED EXPENSES

Fixed expenses generally remain relatively consistent from period to period. Examples include rent, loan payments, and some subscription payments.

VARIABLE EXPENSES

Variable expenses can change from period to period. Examples include groceries, entertainment, transportation, and dining.

SAVING

Saving means setting aside money rather than spending it immediately. Savings can be used for emergencies, planned purchases, and future financial goals.

BUDGET

A budget is a plan for how money will be allocated between spending, saving, debt payments, and other financial goals.

CASH FLOW

Cash flow describes money moving into and out of a person's finances during a period.

POSITIVE CASH FLOW

Positive cash flow occurs when money coming in exceeds money going out during a period.

NEGATIVE CASH FLOW

Negative cash flow occurs when money going out exceeds money coming in during a period.

FINANCIAL GOALS

Financial goals are specific objectives related to money. Examples include building savings, paying down debt, purchasing a home, or investing for long-term goals.
