BUDGETING

Budgeting is the process of planning how income will be used during a specific period.

A budget helps a person understand how much money is coming in, how much is being spent, and how much can be saved or invested.

INCOME

Income can include salary, wages, freelance income, business income, investment income, and other regular sources of money.

EXPENSES

Expenses are the costs of goods and services.

FIXED EXPENSES

Fixed expenses generally remain similar from month to month.

Examples include:
- Rent
- Loan payments
- Insurance
- Some subscriptions

VARIABLE EXPENSES

Variable expenses can change from month to month.

Examples include:
- Groceries
- Dining
- Entertainment
- Transportation
- Shopping

NEEDS AND WANTS

Needs are expenses required for basic living and financial obligations.

Wants are expenses that improve comfort or enjoyment but are not essential.

Separating needs from wants can help identify areas where spending could potentially be reduced.

MONTHLY BUDGET

A simple monthly budget can include:

- Monthly income
- Essential expenses
- Non-essential expenses
- Debt payments
- Savings
- Investments

EXPENSE TRACKING

Tracking expenses helps identify spending patterns.

Reviewing transactions regularly can show where money is being spent and whether actual spending matches the planned budget.

BUDGET REVIEW

A budget should be reviewed periodically because income, expenses, and financial goals can change over time.
